About

My name is Aylin Muzaffarli. I am very interested in physics and currently self-teaching aerospace. I created this website as part of my learning process.

Trajectory is a tool that lets you calculate personalized rocket missions and explore the underlying physics.

To understand these formulas, I first studied the necessary calculus independently, since it is not taught in high school. While in 9th grade, I learned university-level calculus on my own.

In the Library section, you can see my personal calculus notes and the formulas my calculator uses.
